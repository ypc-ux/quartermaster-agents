# VOICE.md — Julius Young III content voice (TASK-060)

The content rules for every draft this engine produces. Public-safe distill of the
local `INFLUENCE_BIBLE.md` + `DESIGN.md` (those live only on the CEO's checkout).

## The rules
- 6th-grade reading level. Short sentences. No fluff.
- Numbers beat adjectives. "I sent 847 cold emails" beats "I sent a lot."
- Every claim gets a receipt: a number, a name, a screenshot, a date.
- Direct, not polite. State things. Don't soften them.
- Every piece delivers at least one of: a laugh, a lesson, or a look behind the curtain. Ideally two.

## Banned words (hard fail — scan every draft)
revolutionary, seamless, unlock, empower, game-changing, cutting-edge, unleash,
elevate, supercharge, leverage (as a verb), disruptive, innovative, best-in-class,
world-class, industry-leading
